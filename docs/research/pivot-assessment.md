## Honest scoring (J, built on rows A1-A4)
1. Rate each criterion separately, never one composite. PCEF (A2) outputs per-criterion ratings, lists weighting as future work, and could not rate determinism, interfaces or stability from its event log.
2. Each rating carries a state: public-site fact (form structured or free text, channels, booking widget), company-supplied (volume, exceptions, error cost, systems), measured, or "not assessed". Volume, exceptions, stability and error cost are never filled from the website.
3. Gate order (a four-step method cited in A1: eligibility, potential, relevance, classification). A process that is not standardised and stable gets "not yet" before any tool is named.
4. n8n is not UI-RPA (J). The RPA literature counts access to many applications as a plus. For n8n each system needs an API, webhook or export, so ask that per system.

## Reusable question sets (J, own design; license it MIT or CC BY)
- T1 Criteria sheet: one question per PCEF criterion; answer is a number, a choice or "don't know"; each answer carries its provenance label. Credit Wellmann et al. (CC BY 4.0).
- T2 SIPOC, filled by the company in 4-5 steps.
- T3 "Last 10 cases" table: received at, channel, first reply at, minutes (min/typical/max), exception and why, systems touched and whether each has an API.
- T4 Data screen: personal-data categories, Art. 9 data, recipients and third countries, whether a person decides (Art. 22), retention.

## Pitfalls
1. The website cannot supply most criteria. Without log data PCEF could not rate determinism, interfaces or stability, so "not assessed" must be a result, never a zero (https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.10900).
2. There is no universal haircut for self-reported minutes. Self-reports and logs correlate only moderately, but that is media use, so transfer to office work is inference (https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nathum/v5y2021i11d10.1038_s41562-021-01117-5.html). Underestimation is common in engineering/management studies but not in psychology studies (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:22061688%20AND%20SRC:MED&resultType=core&format=json). Use ranges plus a measured sample.
3. Small samples mislead (D4). Show "measured" only with N and range; below N=20 stay descriptive (spec P5).
4. Interviews miss exceptions (D1, secondary). T3 asks for the worst case in the last 10.
5. AI adds review overhead and automation bias (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06087). Never use vendor or case-study figures (Lacity 200%, Anthropic 10-15%) as calculator inputs.
6. Auto-qualifying mortgage or insurance enquiries can fall under Art. 22 (G2). Recommend a person who genuinely decides.
7. Real enquiries pasted as "examples" are personal data, and staff-level timings are employee data. Works council co-determines devices "intended to monitor" behaviour or performance (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__87.html), so keep role-level timings.
8. n8n stores execution data by default (success: all; prune at 336 h or 10,000 runs), so recipes must set retention (https://docs.n8n.io/hosting/configuration/environment-variables/executions/).
