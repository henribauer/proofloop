## Gaps (judgment, based on the vendors above only, not the whole market)

**Real, but narrow:**
- **Evidence-linked findings from a public journey.** Every vendor above works from a prompt the user writes, or from internal event or desktop data. None starts from a public website.
- **Break-even with labelled inputs.** UiPath's cost-benefit works from user-entered inputs but documents no ROI formula. Celonis and Signavio lean on marketing savings figures. Zapier Canvas shows "estimated time saved", so the real distinction is "freed time is never cash".
- **Measured before/after for SMEs.** UiPath's baseline-then-actuals exists only in an enterprise RPA tool.

**Not gaps:**
- AI drafting a workflow from a description. Zapier, Make, n8n and Power Automate all do it.
- An importable n8n file on its own. The gap is a blueprint traceable to findings, not the file.
- "Which tools are needed". Builders pick nodes and connectors already.
- A free maturity check for SMEs. Digitalcheck exists.
- An ROI-style audit. The market already sells them.

## Pitfalls

1. **n8n licence.** Consultants may build on their own instance if clients "only receive the outputs". Hosting n8n for clients, or letting outsiders build or configure workflows "through the editor, API, MCP, or an AI agent", needs a commercial licence. A Proofloop app that pushes workflows into a client's n8n is a grey zone: ask license@n8n.io. https://support.n8n.io/article/can-i-use-your-license-for-my-use-case
2. **Blueprint leakage.** Exported n8n JSON "include[s] credential names and IDs"; HTTP nodes imported from cURL may carry auth headers. Scrub blueprints. https://docs.n8n.io/workflows/export-import/
3. **Vendor AI is beta or preview and can be wrong.** Maia carries "verify as errors may occur"; the n8n Assistant says it "can make mistakes". Label any generated blueprint as unreviewed. https://help.make.com/maia-by-make-system-card?hl=en
4. **GDPR routing.** Power Automate Copilot is on by default with cross-geo data sharing outside UK, Australia, US and India, and has "limited support" for languages other than English (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/copilot-overview). n8n Cloud states hardware and data are "currently hosted in the European Union" (https://n8n.io/legal/security/); Frankfurt rests on a community post only. Zapier's EU or DPA position is unverified.
5. **Prices and credits move or contradict.** n8n credit figures conflict, Make prices did not render, and Zapier says "prices can change". Show "seen YYYYMMDD" beside any price and do not hard-code vendor prices into the calculator.
6. **Public-offer timing.** The Berlin centre ended 20260930 and automation-specialist centres start May 2027, after v1 (20270115). A "free help is available" link could be dead. The BMWE press release itself is unverified, as noted in the table.
7. **Vendor savings stats are marketing.** Celonis and Signavio figures have no methodology. Do not quote them as benchmarks.
8. **Do not claim a category.** Paid and free "automation audits with ROI" already exist. Position as an evidence-labelled variant.
